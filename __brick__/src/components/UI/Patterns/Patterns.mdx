# Patterns

Some things look like components but are not: they have no state, no
accessibility contract and no token decision of their own. They are
**compositions** of primitives, and writing them down here is what stops three
slightly different versions from appearing across the codebase.

The rule that decides which is which:

> A component enters the design system if it encapsulates **behaviour** (state,
> accessibility, interaction) or a **token decision that must not drift**.
> Layout and style alone are a recipe.

## Card

There is no `Card` component. A card is `Surface` + `Stack` + `Typography`.

Compose it once in the app layer (e.g. `components/AppCard`) and render every
card through that. If you need a card somewhere new, call that component — do
not re-compose it.

```tsx
<Surface tone="neutral" radius="card" padding="md" elevation="raised">
  <Stack gap="md">
    <Typography variant="heading-5" weight="semibold" as="h3">
      {title}
    </Typography>
    <Typography variant="body-2" tone="muted">
      {content}
    </Typography>
    <Button width="responsive">{cta}</Button>
  </Stack>
</Surface>
```

## Info card

An icon beside one or more label/value pairs (an address, opening hours, a
contact).

```tsx
<Surface tone="success" radius="info-card" padding="sm">
  <Inline gap="md" align="start" wrap={false}>
    <Icon icon={Icons.Location} size="xl" />
    <Stack gap="sm">
      <Typography variant="body-2" weight="bold">{label}</Typography>
      <Typography variant="body-2" weight="medium">{value}</Typography>
    </Stack>
  </Inline>
</Surface>
```

## Error page

`ErrorPageLayout`, next to the two pages that use it. A centred surface with a
pretitle, a title and one recovery action.

## What stays a component

`Dialog`, `Tooltip`, `Accordion`, `ToggleGroup`, `Carousel`, `ExpandableText`, `Select`, `Checkbox`, `RadioGroup`, `Form`, the field
wrappers, `Header` (its mobile menu) and `ThemeToggle`: all of them own state, focus
management or an accessibility contract.

`Typography`, `Badge`, `Button`, `Icon`, `Separator`, `Alert`: each is a token
decision that must not drift.
